IA News Reels

Daily AI news, in short vertical videos.

IA News Reels is a news account about artificial intelligence. Every day it turns the most relevant AI news into short vertical videos in Brazilian Portuguese, aimed at a general, non-technical audience.

How it works

  • Our software collects AI news from public sources and drafts a short script.
  • A person reviews and approves every video before it is published.
  • Approved videos are published to our own accounts, including our TikTok account @ianewsreels.

About the TikTok integration

The integration is an internal tool. It is used only by the owner of the account to publish videos to @ianewsreels. It is not offered to the public, and visitors of this site do not need to sign in or create an account.
