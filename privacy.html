Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Summary

IA News Reels does not collect personal data from visitors of this website and does not offer accounts or sign-in to the public. The only personal data our software handles is the authorization of the account owner's own TikTok account, described below.

2. How our TikTok integration works

We use TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to publish our own videos to our own account, @ianewsreels. The account owner signs in with TikTok once and authorizes the app. The app then uploads videos that a person has approved.

3. Data we access

  • Basic profile information of the authorized account (such as display name and avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to that account.

We do not access the accounts, videos, messages or personal data of other TikTok users.

4. How we store and use it

The access tokens that TikTok issues to the app are stored on a private computer controlled by the account owner and are never published or shared. We use them only to publish our own videos. We do not use this data for advertising, profiling or analytics about other people, and we do not sell, rent or share it with anyone.

5. Website visitors

This website has no forms, no cookies of our own and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address) under GitHub's own privacy statement.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app settings, under the list of apps and permissions connected to the account. When access is revoked, we delete the stored tokens. Anyone who wants to ask about data related to them can write to the address below.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
